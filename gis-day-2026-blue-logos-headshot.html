Geography and Environmental Studies students and faculty

Bringing students, GIS professionals and industry leaders together to connect, learn and explore the future of GIS.

A full day of presentations, professional panels, student projects, networking and career connections — and a look at how GIS is being used to solve real-world challenges beyond the classroom.
